A Minecraft Bedrock add-on featuring the Demon Maw—a deadly living pit that drags players and mobs into its jaws before revealing a treasure-filled stomach.

***

## <span style="color:#e03e2d">Important</span>

Activate both **Maw of Despair BP** and its required **Maw of Despair RP** in your world.

The encounter permanently modifies terrain when it creates the pit, throat, and stomach. Back up any important world before installing the add-on.

***

## Overview

Maw of Despair adds a survival-horror boss encounter that can emerge from natural terrain across the Overworld.

The Demon Maw lies dormant beneath the surface until an adventurer comes close. After a short warning, the ground collapses into a stepped pit and an unnatural force begins dragging everything toward the creature waiting at its center.

Fight from inside the danger zone to force the Maw open, defeat it before the suction becomes overwhelming, and descend through its throat to recover the equipment of adventurers it swallowed long ago.

![Demon Maw encounter](PASTE_ENCOUNTER_IMAGE_OR_GIF_URL_HERE)

***

## Natural Encounter

The Demon Maw is designed to become part of a normal Survival world.

* Natural spawn attempts begin after **10–20 minutes**
* It can appear on suitable land in **any Overworld biome**
* Water, lava, tree canopies, and common constructed surfaces are avoided
* The encounter is generated away from world spawn and near an active Survival player
* Each world generates one Demon Maw encounter
* A dormant Maw remains in the world until it is discovered

***

## The Fight

### 1. Survive the Collapse

Approaching the dormant Maw triggers a warning before the ground falls away in stages, creating a large sloped pit.

Players, animals, and hostile mobs can all be dragged toward the center.

### 2. Enter the Pit to Attack

The Demon Maw seals itself while no player is inside the pit and cannot be harmed in that state.

Enter the pit to force its jaws open. Once exposed, the Maw becomes vulnerable—but you must fight while resisting its pull and avoiding the bite at the center.

![Demon Maw battle](PASTE_BATTLE_IMAGE_OR_GIF_URL_HERE)

### 3. Adapt to the Suction

The pull changes strength in irregular pulses, making movement difficult to predict. It also becomes progressively stronger as the Demon Maw loses health.

Getting too close to the center causes heavy bite damage. Every living creature caught inside the mouth is damaged independently.

### 4. Defeat the Demon Maw

The Demon Maw has **80 HP**. Killing it immediately stops the suction and awards a **Demon Maw Core** as a trophy.

***

## Inside the Stomach

Defeating the Demon Maw opens a physical passage beneath its body.

Climb down the ladder through its throat into a fleshy stomach chamber hidden deep underground. There is **no teleportation and no time limit**, and the same ladder leads back to the surface.

Three barrels inside the stomach receive independent randomized loot rolls.

![Inside the Demon Maw](PASTE_STOMACH_IMAGE_OR_GIF_URL_HERE)

Possible rewards include:

* Iron and gold ingots
* Emeralds, arrows, food, and torches
* Ender pearls, bows, compasses, saddles, and name tags
* Iron swords and diamonds
* Rare golden apples
* An extremely rare enchanted golden apple

The Demon Maw can digest adventurers—but not everything they carried.

## Features

* Original animated Demon Maw model with closed, biting, and defeated states
* Dynamic boss bar, growls, bite sounds, and active suction audio
* Naturally generated collapsing arena
* Unpredictable suction that grows stronger during the fight
* Interaction with players, mobs, and animals
* Persistence across world reloads and player respawns
* Physical throat and treasure-filled stomach beneath the encounter
* Singleplayer and multiplayer support
* English and Thai language support

***

## Compatibility

* Minecraft Bedrock **26.30 or newer**
* Requires both the included Behavior Pack and Resource Pack
